Our Services

Cadastral Surveying

Cadastral surveys involve the precise measurement and mapping of land parcels to establish legal boundaries for ownership, title registration, and land transfer. These surveys form the basis for acquiring title deeds and resolving boundary disputes, ensuring compliance with national land laws. Elgon United Surveyors and Engineers uses modern GPS and Total Station equipment to generate accurate survey plans and beacon certificates, helping clients secure their land rights confidently and legally.

Boundary Opening & Re-establishment

This service involves identifying, verifying, and physically marking property boundaries based on existing legal records or past survey plans. It is commonly needed in cases of boundary disputes, missing beacons, or prior to construction or fencing. By retrieving original survey data from national records and using advanced surveying tools, we restore boundaries with precision and issue formal boundary opening reports, giving clients peace of mind and legal clarity.

Subdivision Surveys

Subdivision surveys are essential for dividing large land parcels into smaller plots for sale, inheritance, or development purposes. Our team assists with planning, county approvals, beacon installation, and preparation of mutation forms and final survey plans. These services are critical for real estate developers, families distributing land, or individuals seeking to formalize plot ownership and register new titles with the Lands Office.

Engineering Surveys

Engineering surveys provide vital spatial data for planning, designing, and executing construction projects, including roads, drainage systems, buildings, and utilities. We deliver accurate elevation profiles, layout plans, and as-built data using GNSS receivers, Total Stations, and other modern tools. Our surveys ensure that construction aligns with approved designs, supporting engineers, contractors, and developers in delivering safe and compliant infrastructure.

Topographic Surveys

Topographic surveys capture the natural and man-made features of land, including elevation changes, terrain contours, vegetation, and existing structures. These surveys are crucial for site planning, architectural design, environmental assessment, and civil works. We generate detailed contour maps, digital elevation models, and georeferenced drawings using drone mapping and ground-based instruments, enabling informed decision-making for development projects.
