FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What services does EUSE provide?

We specialize in land surveying, engineering consultation, mapping, boundary demarcation, topographic surveys, GIS services, and construction supervision.

2. How do I request a land survey?

You can request a survey by filling out our contact form or calling our office directly. Our team will get in touch to schedule a site visit and provide a quotation.

3. Are your services recognized by the government?

Yes. EUSE is fully licensed and operates in accordance with national land and engineering regulations. We also collaborate with local authorities and land boards.

4. How long does a typical land survey take?

The duration depends on the size and complexity of the land. Most standard residential plots take 1–3 days, while larger or more technical projects may take longer.

5. Do you offer internships for students?

Yes. We provide internship opportunities for university students pursuing land surveying, civil engineering, and related fields. Please see our internship section for details.

6. How can I contact EUSE?

You can reach us via email, phone, or by visiting our offices. Visit the Contacts page for full details.
