About Us

ELGON UNITED SURVEYORS AND ENGINEERS is a multi-disciplinary firm dedicated to delivering high-quality services in land surveying, architectural design, and engineering consultancy. With a focus on precision, innovation, and client satisfaction, we bring your vision to life from concept to completion.

Our team comprises licensed professionals and experienced technicians who ensure compliance with industry standards while delivering creative and functional solutions. Whether it’s a boundary survey, building design, or engineering project management, EUSE is your trusted partner in development and infrastructure.
